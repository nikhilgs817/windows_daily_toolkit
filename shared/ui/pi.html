Monitor Control

Looking for screens…
When turning off, also put the monitor itself to sleep (DDC/CI), and wake it when turning on. Monitors without DDC/CI are just disconnected.
The key shows what a press will do: red TURN OFF while the screen is on, green TURN ON while it is off. Off = disconnected in Windows (windows move to your other screens). The main display can be turned off when another screen is active. The last active screen stays on.

Apps page

Opens the Apps page: a Back key plus your app keys. To add an app, open the Apps profile in the Stream Deck app and drag Open App… (or any key) onto an empty slot. Apps with their own keys (VS Code) switch to them; the others keep you on the Apps page.

Website Folder

Opens your editable Websites page. Add Open Website… keys in the Stream Deck editor and enter each URL. Keep the Back key to return to your main keys.

Back

Returns to your main keys from the Apps or Websites collection.
